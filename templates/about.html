Our Story

Quality products and reliable technology solutions

IT Eric is a leading IT hardware and solutions provider in Kerala, specializing in computer and laptop sales, computer components, and professional IT services. With six branches across Kerala, we serve individuals, businesses, and corporate clients with quality products and reliable technology solutions.

We offer a wide range of IT products, including desktops, laptops, processors, motherboards, storage devices, and computer accessories. Our product portfolio includes leading technology brands such as Intel, AMD, HP, Dell, Gigabyte, WD, and Seagate.

Along with IT product sales, we provide computer assembly, laptop and desktop repairs, hardware upgrades, troubleshooting, and corporate IT support, including Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC).

With a focus on quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction, we aim to provide the right technology solutions for every customer.
